// Thus Spoke The Donke

> About Thus Spoke the Donke

Thus Spoke the Donke is an immersive stealth-action sandbox where you play as a mercenary assassin serving the will of The Great Donke. Customize weapons and abilities, build your own combat style, and complete contracts your way, through precision stealth or brutal, environment-driven combat.

"The Donke does not care how you succeed. Only that you do."

Core Pillars

  • Freedom of Approach: Infiltrate quietly, cause chaos, or manipulate distractions in dynamic sandboxes.
  • Loadout Customization: Craft personalized weapons, wall-piercing shots, and tactical gadgets.
  • Sharp Narrative & Satire: Question authority and fanatical beliefs in a distinct retro-futuristic empire.

Controls

Mouse & Keyboard / Full Gamepad Support

Supported Languages

TEXT & UI: English, Turkish, French, Italian, German, Spanish - Spain, Portuguese - Brazil, Spanish - Latin America, Portuguese - Portugal, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Russian, Polish
