About Wise Monke

Wise Monke Entertainment Studio is an independent game development team passionate about building games with distinct personalities and responsive mechanics. We believe games should be memorable, surprising, and fun right from the moment you pick up the controller.

From satirical stealth sandboxes questioning blind loyalty to chess games that redefine piece movement and cosmic planet pulverizers, we craft experiences that we genuinely want to play ourselves. Every pixel and every system serves a clear purpose.

We build closely with our community on Discord, gathering feedback early and often during development.
