Mate'Morphosis

> About Mate'Morphosis

BECOME WHAT YOU CAPTURE.
Mate'Morphosis is a chess puzzle game where you navigate one piece to capture the enemy queen. Every capture transforms you into the piece you just took. Plan moves ahead, plot sequences, and master evolving puzzle mechanics.

Developed in collaboration with Bruder Studio.

Key Features

  • Morph: Pawns become knights, knights become bishops — movement evolves with every capture.
  • Evolving Mechanics: Surprises beyond classic chess keep boards exciting from start to Mate.
  • Hand-crafted Challenge: Steady difficulty curve with clean, readable pixel art.
  • Instant Learning Loop: Snappy restarts and fast puzzle pacing.

Controls

Mouse & Keyboard / Gamepad & Touch Support
